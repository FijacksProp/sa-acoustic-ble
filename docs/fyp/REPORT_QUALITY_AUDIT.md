# Project Report Quality Audit

## Scope

This audit covers the final Chapters One to Five Markdown sources and the
generated Microsoft Word report:

`Smart_Attendance_System_Final_Project_Report.docx`

The earlier `Smart_Attendance_Report_Chapters_1_to_3.docx` remains a historical
draft and should not be submitted in place of the final report.

## Academic Alignment

- The report title, author name, matriculation number, department, institution,
  and implemented acoustic/BLE scope are consistent across the preliminary
  pages and all five chapters.
- The aim is supported by five objectives. Chapter Three explains how each
  objective was implemented, Chapter Four reports the available evidence, and
  Chapter Five evaluates achievement without introducing a new objective.
- BLE is described as the principal practical signal path in the current
  prototype. Acoustic proof is described as supplementary very-short-range
  evidence.
- The fixed DX-CP27 device is described as a room beacon, not a Bluetooth
  amplifier. Its static identity is constrained by room, open-session, and RSSI
  rules.
- Device binding, duplicate prevention, and replay control are described as
  safeguards rather than absolute proof of physical identity.
- Small-scale observations are separated from automated test results. Estimated
  ranges are not converted into unsupported success percentages.

## Source and Citation Checks

- Fifteen reference entries are included.
- Every reference is cited in at least one chapter.
- Every detected author-date citation maps to an entry in the reference list.
- DOI and publisher links are recorded in `REFERENCES.md` and the source
  verification register.
- No long quotation or copied source passage is used. Related studies are
  paraphrased and compared against the implemented project.

## Word-Document Checks

- A4 paper with one-inch margins.
- Three document sections: unnumbered title page, lower-Roman preliminary pages,
  and Arabic-numbered main text.
- Times New Roman throughout; 12-point double-spaced body text.
- APA-style numbered heading hierarchy, table titles, figure captions, figure
  notes, and hanging-indent references.
- Automatic table of contents, list of figures, list of tables, and page fields
  updated in Microsoft Word.
- Eleven document tables, including nine numbered report tables.
- Five embedded images: the University of Ilorin logo and four
  implementation-derived technical figures.
- No comments, tracked insertions, tracked deletions, authored footnotes,
  authored endnotes, draft markers, or insertion placeholders.
- The final Word render produces 84 A4 pages, including preliminary pages and
  70 Arabic-numbered main-text pages.

## Items Requiring Human Completion or Further Evidence

- The supervisor, Head of Department, external examiner, signatures, and dates
  must be completed by the appropriate persons.
- Final Android interface screenshots should be captured from the release APK
  with anonymised demonstration data before they are inserted.
- Broader field testing should use fixed trial counts, measured distances,
  recorded RSSI/noise conditions, more phones, full-class concurrency, and
  adjacent-room sessions before stronger performance claims are made.
- Any department-specific formatting instruction issued later by the supervisor
  takes precedence over the current APA-informed project-report layout.
